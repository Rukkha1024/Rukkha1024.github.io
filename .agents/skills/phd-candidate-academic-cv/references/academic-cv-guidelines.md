# Academic CV guideline basis

Checked 2026-08-14 through the repository's Aside-only verification route.

## Harvard Griffin GSAS

Source: [GSAS CV & Cover Letter Guide](https://careerservices.fas.harvard.edu/resources/gsas-cv-cover-letter-guide-2/)

- Use a CV to represent academic accomplishments and experience.
- Include contact information, education, research, teaching, publications,
  presentations, honors, awards, and references when applicable.
- Do not treat one template as universally correct. Order categories by
  importance and follow the conventions of the field.
- Put important information on page one, keep dates and locations toward the
  right, use a common 10-12 point font, and describe work with active verbs and
  concise fragments.
- Keep an archival master and tailor copies to their audience. Harvard notes
  that graduate-student CVs commonly occupy two to five pages.

## MIT Career Advising and Professional Development

Source: [Curricula vitae (CVs)](https://capd.mit.edu/resources/cvs/)

- Maintain an authoritative CV containing the full scholarly record and make
  tailored copies for particular opportunities.
- There is no single correct format. Optimize for skimming with consistent
  typography, headings, and white space.
- Put the most important material early. Describe the goal, contribution, and
  result of research experience rather than listing duties alone.
- Include education, research, publications, teaching, outreach, and technical
  skills when supported. List publications in reverse chronological order,
  use the field's citation style, and emphasize the applicant's name.
- MIT describes two to four pages as typical for a younger professional.

## Applied master-CV decisions

- Use a two-page, single-column, research-first master CV for this early-career
  applicant.
- Use restrained black typography, explicit section hierarchy, right-aligned
  dates, real bullets, and no decorative boxes, photo, or graphic skill bars.
- Preserve the master as general graduate-application evidence. Make a separate
  tailored copy only when a program explicitly requires it.
